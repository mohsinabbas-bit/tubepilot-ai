Privacy Policy for Tubepilot Ai

1. Data Collection & Usage: Tubepilot Ai accesses YouTube account information using Google OAuth 2.0 to provide channel management features directly within our desktop application.

2. Data Storage & Security: All access tokens and channel data are processed and stored locally on your desktop machine. We do not store, transfer, or sell your personal YouTube data to any external servers or third parties.

3. Google API Services Compliance: Tubepilot Ai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. User Control & Data Deletion: Users can revoke Tubepilot Ai's access to their YouTube data at any time via Google Security Settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
