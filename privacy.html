Privacy Policy for TubePilot AI

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

TubePilot AI ("TubePilot AI," "we," "our," or "us") operates the TubePilot AI desktop application for Windows and the website at https://tubepilotai.app (together, the "Service" or "API Client"). This Privacy Policy explains how we access, use, store, and protect information, including Google user data obtained through Google OAuth and YouTube API Services.

By using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

1. Application Overview

TubePilot AI is a Windows desktop application that helps YouTube creators and agencies connect authorized YouTube channels, manage upload queues, schedule publishing, generate metadata assistance, and view channel performance inside the app. The app communicates with Google only when you choose to sign in with Google and use YouTube-related features. Optional Team and Always-On features may also sync job or collaboration data through cloud services you enable by using those features.

2. Device Storage, Cookies, and Similar Technologies (YouTube Developer Policy III.A.2g)

Disclosure required by YouTube API Services Developer Policies, Section III.A.2g: TubePilot AI (the API Client) stores, accesses, and collects information directly and indirectly on and from users' devices, and allows certain third parties to do so where needed to operate sign-in and Service features. This includes placing, accessing, or recognizing cookies or similar technology on users' devices or browsers (for example browser cookies during Google sign-in, local application storage files, and browser localStorage / similar client-side storage).

In more detail:

  • Desktop application storage on your device: TubePilot AI stores and accesses information on your Windows device so the app can function. This may include Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, connected channel identifiers, upload queues and schedules, app settings and preferences, local caches (such as thumbnails or research results), UI state, and related application data files in the app's user-data folders and related local paths (for example Windows userData / Documents folders used by the app).
  • Cookies and similar browser technologies: When you sign in with Google through a browser window opened by the app, Google may place, access, or recognize cookies or similar technologies on your browser or device as part of Google's authentication flow. Our website and in-app web views may also use similar technologies such as browser localStorage, session storage, or equivalent client-side storage to remember UI preferences (for example sidebar state) and keep the client working smoothly.
  • Website (tubepilotai.app): The marketing website does not require Google sign-in to browse. It may still use ordinary browser/client storage or similar technology as needed for basic site function (for example remembering menu or display preferences). We do not use the website to collect YouTube account data from casual visitors. If you click through to Google, Microsoft Store, or other third-party pages, those parties may set their own cookies under their policies.
  • Third parties that may store or access information on or from your device:
    • Google / YouTube: During OAuth sign-in and while using YouTube API Services, Google may store or access cookies or similar technology and process API requests involving your authorized data.
    • Cloud backend used for Team / Always-On features (for example Supabase-hosted APIs): If you use Team or Always-On features, job and collaboration-related information needed for those features may be sent from your device to that cloud service so uploads and team workflows can continue when configured.
    • OpenAI (optional, user-controlled): Only if you enter your own OpenAI API key and use optional AI features; content you choose to process may leave your device to OpenAI under your key.
    • Microsoft Store / Windows: Purchase, license, and update flows may involve Microsoft technologies on your device under Microsoft's policies.

These device-storage and cookie/similar technologies are used to authenticate you, keep you signed in, operate queues and schedules, remember preferences, call YouTube API Services on your behalf, and support optional Team / Always-On / AI features you enable. They are not used by TubePilot AI to sell your Google user data or to serve third-party advertising inside the API Client.

3. Google OAuth Scopes We Request

When you connect a Google or YouTube account, the OAuth consent screen lists the permissions (scopes) required for app features. TubePilot AI may request the following scopes:

  • openid, email, profile — Identify your Google account during sign-in and associate the correct YouTube channels with your session in the app.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — Read channel and video information (for example titles, descriptions, tags, statistics, and channel metadata) to display dashboards, analytics, research, and insights inside the app.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — Upload video files and manage uploads that you initiate in the app (for example queued or scheduled uploads to your channel).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — Update video metadata and manage YouTube content that you edit through the app (for example changing title, description, tags, or privacy status on videos you manage).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — Manage your YouTube account features that require broader channel and video management permissions when you use upload, scheduling, metadata edit, and related in-app workflows.

We request only scopes needed for features you use. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions or at Google security settings.

4. Google User Data We Access

Through Google OAuth and YouTube Data API Services, the application may access the following categories of Google user data (depending on which features you use):

  • Google account identifiers: Basic profile information from your Google account (such as account email and profile identifiers) used to link your sign-in to connected channels.
  • YouTube channel information: Channel IDs, channel titles, thumbnails, branding, subscriber counts, view counts, and other channel metadata for channels you authorize.
  • Video metadata and listings: Video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags, categories, privacy or publish status (Public, Private, or Unlisted), upload timestamps, and thumbnails for videos on channels you manage or analyze through in-app tools.
  • Video performance and analytics data: Statistics such as views, likes, comments counts, and related metrics displayed in dashboards, analytics, channel research, and insights features.
  • Upload and content-management data: Video files and metadata that you select in the app for upload, scheduling, review, or metadata updates to YouTube.
  • OAuth tokens: Access and refresh tokens issued by Google so the app can call YouTube API Services on your behalf until you sign out or revoke access. These tokens are stored on your device as described in Section 2.

We do not request access to unrelated Google products (such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, or Google Calendar) for core YouTube management features.

5. How We Use Google User Data

We use Google user data only to provide and operate the Service features you request. Specific uses include:

  • Authentication and channel connection: Sign you in with Google OAuth and connect YouTube channels you select.
  • Dashboard and channel management: Display connected channels, queue status, and summary metrics.
  • Uploads and scheduling: Upload videos and apply publish settings you configure, including Public / Private / Unlisted visibility and review or automatic workflows you enable.
  • Metadata editing: Read and update titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, and related video fields when you edit or publish content in the app.
  • Analytics and research: Show performance metrics, comparisons, and insights derived from YouTube API data to help you understand channel activity. Cached performance snapshots kept on your device are limited in retention (see Section 7).
  • Quota and reliability: Monitor API usage limits to pause or protect operations when daily API quota is low (as configured in Settings).
  • Support and troubleshooting: Diagnose errors you report (for example upload failures or authentication issues) using information you provide and local app logs.

Limited use commitment (Google API Services User Data Policy):

  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving user-facing YouTube management features in TubePilot AI.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to operate the Service (see Sections 2 and 8), comply with law, or with your direction (for example uploading content to YouTube at your request).

6. Optional Third-Party AI Features (User-Controlled)

If you optionally enter an OpenAI API key in Settings, metadata or video-analysis features may send content you choose to process (such as video filenames, extracted frames, or text you generate) directly from your device to OpenAI using your API key and your OpenAI account. That data is not sent to TubePilot AI marketing servers. OpenAI processing is governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. You can disable these features by removing your API key.

7. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Local-first storage: As disclosed in Section 2, OAuth tokens, channel settings, queues, preferences, caches, and most app data are stored on your Windows device in local application storage.
  • Cloud sync for optional features: Team / Always-On related job or collaboration records may be stored in the configured cloud backend so those features can run as designed. This is limited to data needed for those features.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell Google user data or YouTube API data.
  • Retention: We retain Google user data locally only as long as needed to provide the Service while your account remains connected and the app is in use, or until you delete local app data or uninstall the application. Cached YouTube performance statistics kept for in-app insights are purged on a rolling basis (approximately 30 days) and are not kept indefinitely for secondary purposes.
  • Deletion and revocation: You may sign out in the app, delete local channel data, uninstall TubePilot AI, and revoke the app in Google Account permissions. After revocation, the app cannot access your YouTube data unless you connect again.
  • Google revocation (Authorized Data): In addition to TubePilot AI's normal deletion procedures, you can revoke the API Client's access to your data at any time via the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also available at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

8. Data Protection and Security Mechanisms

We protect sensitive data using the following measures:

  • Encryption in transit: All communication between TubePilot AI and Google API Services (including YouTube Data API) uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local credential storage: OAuth tokens and app secrets configured on your device are stored locally for app operation and are not published by us to third-party marketing databases.
  • Least-privilege access: The app requests OAuth scopes tied to YouTube management features and uses API access only for authorized channels you connect.
  • User control: You choose which channels to connect, which videos to upload, and when to publish or edit metadata (including Public / Private / Unlisted).
  • Access revocation: You can remove app access at any time via Google Account / security permissions (links in Section 7).
  • Quota protection: Configurable limits and auto-pause options help reduce accidental excessive API usage.
  • Device security: You are responsible for securing your Windows user account, disk encryption, and physical access to your computer where local tokens and video files are stored.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect information using reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a local desktop application with optional cloud features.

9. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information only in these situations:

  • With Google / YouTube: When you use the app to upload, update, or manage content on YouTube at your direction.
  • With service providers needed to operate optional features: For example the cloud backend used for Team / Always-On sync, or OpenAI if you provide an API key for optional AI features.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • Safety: To protect rights, safety, and security of users or the public, where permitted by law.

We do not allow unrelated third parties to serve advertisements inside the API Client using Google user data, and we do not share Google user data with data brokers or for third-party advertising.
